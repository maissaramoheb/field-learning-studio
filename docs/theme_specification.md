# Field Learning Studio — Day / Night Theme Specification

- **Night Theme (Reference)**: Preserves existing v1.0 production palette identically (`#050b14` canvas, `#101b2c` surface, `#2563eb`/`#3b82f6` accent, `#22d3ee` trace, `#f8fafc` text, `#94a3b8` muted).
- **Day Background (Canvas)**: Warm, neutral off-white research canvas `#f6f8fa` (avoids sterile glare while providing high editorial readability).
- **Day Surfaces**: Layered architectural hierarchy — `#ffffff` primary work surface, `#f1f4f8` muted/status rails, `#ffffff` elevated with subtle elevation, `#eff6ff` (soft blue-slate) selected row/card state.
- **Day Text**: Deep editorial ink `#0f172a` primary text (14.2:1 contrast), `#475569` muted text (5.5:1 contrast, WCAG AA compliant), `#64748b` secondary meta/counts.
- **Day Borders**: Hairline cool-gray `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10)` (`#e2e8f0`) base, `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)` (`#cbd5e1`) strong dividers, avoiding excessive card boxing.
- **Day Accent**: Authoritative FLS blue `#2563eb` for primary actions and active indicators, `#1d4ed8` on hover/active, with translucent wash `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`.
- **Day Trace & Lineage**: Legible ocean cyan/sky `#0284c7` (text `#0369a1`, border `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35)`, wash `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`), ensuring provenance IDs remain crisp on light surfaces.
- **Day Warning & Limitations**: Refined warm amber `#d97706` (text `#b45309`, wash `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`, border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`) — soft editorial limitation panel without yellow alert flooding.
- **Day Success & Validation**: Crisp emerald `#059669` (text `#047857`, wash `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, border `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.25)`).
- **Day Selection**: Translucent cyan wash `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.20)` with `#0f172a` text for browser selection.
- **Day Shadows & Elevation**: Restrained directional elevation — cards `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, elevated panels `0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`, dialogs `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Day Document Canvas**: Physical sheet contrast — outer canvas `#eaedf2` surrounding crisp `#ffffff` document preview sheet with subtle shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`), ensuring brief preview never bleeds into background.
