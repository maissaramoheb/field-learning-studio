# v0.9 Evidence Support Profile & Gap Detection Engine

## 1. Overview

The Evidence Support Profile and Gap Detection engine provides deterministic, transparent, and study-aware evaluation of how strongly a finding is corroborated by its supporting evidence base.

> **Important**: The support tier is decision support, not an automated substitute for evaluator judgment. It surfaces empirical patterns, triangulation health, and coverage blind spots so researchers and evaluators can defend their claims with transparency.

---

## 2. Core Analytical Principles

### 2.1 Source Independence Rule
A finding must not conflate the volume of evidence excerpts with genuine triangulation.
- Three evidence entries extracted from `SRC-001` count as **1 independent source**, not 3.
- True triangulation requires distinct `sourceId` values across independent informants, observation sessions, or documentary records.

### 2.2 Study-Aware Scope Calibration
Rather than enforcing arbitrary cross-site or multi-stakeholder penalties, the engine calibrates against the study's declared `StudyScopeConfig`:
- **Single-Site Studies**: When `isSingleSiteStudy === true` (or only 1 target site is configured), evidence from that site satisfies the site-coverage requirement. The finding is not penalized merely because only one location exists.
- **Multi-Site Studies**: Evaluates whether evidence is confined to a single site (e.g., Minya only) while other target sites (e.g., Assiut) remain unrepresented.
- **Stakeholder-Specific Findings**: When `finding.isStakeholderSpecific === true` and `finding.targetStakeholderGroup` is defined (e.g., `"Teachers"`), the engine evaluates whether that specific target group is represented. It does not require unrelated stakeholder groups merely to inflate an artificial diversity score.
- **Broad/General Findings**: When a claim is not stakeholder-specific, multi-stakeholder representation is required, and missing configured groups are flagged.

### 2.3 Contradiction Handling
Contradictory evidence is vital analytical information, not bad data:
- Contradictory evidence is tracked via `finding.contradictoryEvidenceIds`, evidence-level contradiction links, or explicit substantive summaries.
- Any active, unresolved contradiction prevents a finding from being classified as `Strongly Supported` and downgrades the tier to `Emerging`.
- The engine does not autonomously delete or reconcile contradictory data. Evaluators must contextualize or qualify divergence.

---

## 3. Support Tiers

The engine employs three transparent tiers without artificial percentages or fake decimal precision:

| Tier | Heuristic Requirements |
|---|---|
| **Strongly Supported** | • At least 3 independent sources (`independentSourceCount >= 3`)<br>• At least 2 collection methods (`isMultiMethod === true`)<br>• No unresolved contradictions (`hasContradictions === false`)<br>• Adequate site representation relative to study scope (`isSingleSiteStudy` or verified cross-site)<br>• Adequate stakeholder representation relative to claim scope (target group present for specific claims, multi-stakeholder for broad claims) |
| **Partially Supported** | • At least 2 independent sources (`independentSourceCount >= 2`)<br>• No active unresolved contradictions<br>• Method concentration (e.g., 4 interviews but 0 observations), single-site concentration in a multi-site study, or missing a general stakeholder perspective |
| **Emerging** | • Only 1 independent source (single-source dependency)<br>• OR active unresolved contradiction<br>• OR critical representation gap (e.g., 0 sources, 0 sites, or stakeholder-specific claim missing its target group) |

---

## 4. Deterministic Transparency Flags

Every profile includes human-readable factual flags explaining the exact composition of the evidence base:
- `Only 1 independent source` / `3 independent sources identified`
- `All supporting evidence comes from Key Informant Interview (method concentration)`
- `Multi-method triangulation achieved (Key Informant Interview, Direct Observation)`
- `Single-site study: site coverage requirement satisfied`
- `Evidence currently limited to Minya; missing Assiut`
- `Stakeholder-specific claim supported by target group: Teachers`
- `Missing perspective(s): Children`
- `Contradictory evidence remains unresolved (1 item)`

## 5. Evidence Gap Types & Severities

The Gap Detector generates actionable, structured gaps for findings and overall study scope:

| Gap Type | Trigger Condition | Default Severity | Suggested Remediation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`InsufficientCoverage`** | 0 supporting sources | `Critical` | Link verified evidence entries before claiming finding. |
| **`SingleSourceDependency`** | 1 independent source | `Needs Attention` | Gather evidence from at least one additional source. |
| **`MethodConcentration`** | 2+ sources but only 1 collection method | `Needs Attention` | Corroborate with alternative method (observation, FGD, survey). |
| **`MissingStakeholder`** | Specific target group missing OR broad claim missing target perspectives | `Critical` (specific)<br>`Needs Attention` (broad) | Engage missing stakeholder groups to assess generalizability. |
| **`MissingSite`** | Multi-site study with evidence missing configured target sites | `Needs Attention` | Expand fieldwork or qualify claim as site-specific. |
| **`UnresolvedContradiction`** | Linked or substantive contradictory evidence | `Critical` | Document context for divergence or qualify claim boundary. |

---

## 6. Limitations of the Deterministic Model

1. **Syntactic, not Semantic Understanding**: The engine evaluates structural triangulation (source diversity, method count, site distribution, stakeholder alignment) and explicit contradiction links. It does not replace qualitative evaluation of text meaning or nuance.
2. **Dependent on Metadata Quality**: Accurate source typing, location tagging, and stakeholder attribution are prerequisites for reliable support profiles.
3. **Non-Equivalence to Credibility**: A claim supported by two rigorously conducted, exhaustive studies may be analytically sounder than five superficial interviews; human evaluation remains authoritative.
