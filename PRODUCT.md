# Product

## Register

product

## Users
MEL officers, evaluation consultants, NGO/INGO programme managers, donor-reporting teams, learning officers, peacebuilding and social cohesion programme teams. They operate in institutional contexts, often in conflict-sensitive or social-cohesion areas, requiring careful, verified, and traceably-backed claims.

## Product Purpose
Help users turn messy field evidence into traceable findings, lessons learned, good practices, recommendations, QA review, and donor-ready learning briefs.

## Brand Personality
Calm, analytical, institutional, evidence-based, practical.

## Anti-references
- Do not look like a flashy AI startup landing page.
- Do not use purple-gradient AI aesthetics.
- Do not use playful SaaS visuals.
- Do not use heavy glassmorphism.
- Do not use excessive animations.
- Do not make it look like a generic chatbot.

## Design Principles
- **Absolute Traceability**: Every finding, lesson, and recommendation must link back clearly and interactively to its source evidence.
- **Readability & Hierarchy**: Professional document presentation over flashy UI widgets. Focus on clear typography, low cognitive load, and clean document layouts.
- **Verification over Automation**: Emphasize human review and QA safeguards over fully automated text generation. Make QA checks actionable.
- **Credibility & Trust**: Calm, institutional layout fitting for donors and high-level NGO stakeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High readability, high contrast text on backgrounds (compliance with WCAG AA/AAA).
- Support for keyboard navigation and screen readers.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Future support for RTL layouts (e.g., Arabic, Hebrew) due to field locations.
