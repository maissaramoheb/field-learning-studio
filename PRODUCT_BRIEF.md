# Product Brief

## Problem

MEL, evaluation, donor reporting, and programme learning teams often collect rich field evidence in notes, interviews, reports, matrices, and informal summaries. Turning that material into credible learning products is slow, inconsistent, and prone to weak traceability.

## Target Users

- MEL officers and managers.
- Evaluation consultants.
- Programme learning teams.
- NGO and INGO programme staff.
- Donor reporting teams.
- UN-adjacent implementation and learning teams.

## Product Promise

Field Learning Studio helps teams move from messy field evidence to traceable findings, lessons learned, good practices, recommendations, QA review, and donor-ready learning briefs.

## Core Workflow

Raw field evidence -> source inventory -> evidence matrix -> findings -> lessons learned -> good practices -> recommendations -> QA review -> learning brief preview/export.

## Outputs

- Source inventory.
- Evidence matrix.
- Theme-coded evidence.
- Findings.
- Lessons learned.
- Good practices.
- Recommendations.
- QA review.
- Donor-ready learning brief.

## Current Demo Case

Community Bridges Initiative: Youth and Women's Participation in Local Peacebuilding.

This is a fictional case used to demonstrate the workflow safely without real sensitive field evidence.

## Strategic Positioning

Field Learning Studio is positioned as a practical synthesis layer for programme learning. It keeps sense-making ahead of software complexity and makes traceability visible before introducing automation.

## Revenue Direction

1. Productized consulting first.
2. Paid pilots second.
3. SaaS later only after validation.
